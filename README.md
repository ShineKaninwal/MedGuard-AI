## 📌 Overview

MedGuard AI is an AI-powered healthcare management platform designed to help patients manage their medications, prescriptions, health records, reminders, and emergency information from one place.

The platform combines medication management, AI assistance, health tracking, caregiver support, reminders, and emergency features into a single user-friendly dashboard.

The goal is to reduce the difficulty of managing multiple medicines, medical documents, health information, and important healthcare tasks.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Managing healthcare information can become difficult when a person has:

- Multiple medicines
- Different medication schedules
- Prescription documents
- Medical reports
- Doctor appointments
- Medicine refill requirements
- Emergency contacts
- Health measurements
- Questions about symptoms

Important information can easily become scattered across different applications or forgotten.

MedGuard AI provides a centralized platform to make everyday healthcare management simpler and more organized.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

MedGuard AI brings important healthcare management features together in one platform.

Users can:

- Manage medications
- Add medicines manually
- Scan prescriptions
- Track taken and missed medicines
- Set medication reminders
- Set refill reminders
- Track medical checkups
- Store medical reports
- Monitor health information
- Manage emergency contacts
- Access emergency support
- Interact with an AI health assistant
- Share selected medical information through QR
- Allow caregivers to monitor important medication information

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 💊 1. Medication Management

Users can manage their complete medication schedule from the dashboard.

Features include:

- Add medications
- Add dosage information
- Set medication frequency
- Set medication duration
- Track medication status
- Mark medicines as taken
- Monitor missed doses
- Set refill reminders

This helps users maintain a consistent medication routine.

---

## 📷 2. Prescription Scanner

Users can upload or scan a prescription instead of manually entering every medication.

The system can process prescription information and assist with extracting relevant medication details.

Users can also manually edit or add medication information when required.

### Benefits

- Reduces manual data entry
- Makes prescription management easier
- Helps organize medicines
- Saves time

---

## 🤖 3. AI Health Assistant

MedGuard AI includes an AI-powered conversational health assistant.

Users can describe symptoms or ask general healthcare-related questions using natural language.

The assistant can help with:

- Understanding symptoms
- General health information
- Medication-related questions
- Basic guidance
- Identifying situations where professional medical consultation may be appropriate

The AI assistant is designed as a support system and not as a replacement for a qualified doctor.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧 4. Caregiver Support

MedGuard AI provides functionality for caregivers who need to help monitor a patient's medication routine.

Important information can include:

- Medication schedules
- Taken/missed medication status
- Important reminders
- Patient information

This can be useful for elderly patients or people who require assistance managing multiple medications.

---

## 🚨 5. Emergency Support

The platform provides quick access to important emergency information.

Users can store:

- Emergency contacts
- Important patient information
- Relevant health information

An emergency button provides quick access to the configured emergency functionality.

The goal is to make important information easier to access during urgent situations.

---

## ⏰ 6. Smart Reminders

MedGuard AI helps users remember important healthcare activities.

Reminders can include:

- Medication times
- Missed medications
- Medicine refills
- Doctor checkups
- Follow-up appointments
- Other healthcare activities

---

## 🧪 7. Medical Reports

Users can keep track of important medical reports and health documents.

This provides a centralized location for relevant healthcare information.

Instead of searching through multiple files or applications, users can access important information through the platform.

---

## ❤️ 8. Health Tracking

MedGuard AI provides health tracking functionality for important health measurements.

Examples include:

- Heart rate
- Blood pressure
- Other tracked health metrics

The dashboard provides a simple overview of recorded health information.

---

## 📱 9. QR-Based Health Information Sharing

MedGuard AI includes QR-based sharing for selected patient information.

Users can select relevant information and generate a QR code that can be shared with an authorized person.

This can make it easier to provide important information to:

- Caregivers
- Family members
- Healthcare professionals
- Authorized users

Only selected information should be shared.
